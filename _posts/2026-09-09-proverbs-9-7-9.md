Stop burning yourself out trying to fix people who've already decided to mock you for trying. That's not mercy, it's just wasted breath, and the verse says it costs you shame, not credit. But don't use that as an excuse to go quiet on everybody — a wise man, a just man, is worth every ounce of honest correction you can give him, because he'll actually get wiser from it. Read the room before you open your mouth, then don't hold back with the guy who'll listen.
