This isn't a warning about back-alley muggers. It's a warning about the guy at work, in your group chat, or at the bar who makes crooked money sound smart and normal — the padded invoice, the "everybody does it" shortcut, the deal where somebody quietly gets hurt so you can get paid. Verse 10 says the ask will be smooth (*pathah*). Verse 11 says the plan underneath it is still an ambush (*arab*) — somebody unsuspecting eats the cost of your quick money. Solomon's answer isn't "think it over." It's "consent thou not" — a flat, immediate no, decided before the pitch ever gets made to you.
