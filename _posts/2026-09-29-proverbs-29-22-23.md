These two verses are one move, not two separate lessons. An angry man is a man who's already decided his read on things is final — that's pride wearing a red face. The blow-up and the swelled head come from the same root: thinking you're too big to be told no, too right to be interrupted, too important to let it go. One version leaks out loud as strife. The other sits quiet until it gets knocked down. Both end the same way — lower than where they started.
