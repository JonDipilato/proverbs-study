You walk past people every day who are bent over on the inside and showing you nothing on the outside — a coworker, your wife, your kid, the guy at the counter. This verse says the fix for that isn't complicated. It's not a lecture, it's not advice, it's not "it'll be fine." It's one true, specific, good sentence, said to their face. That's the whole weapon. And it works both directions — you're carrying weight too, and you don't have to carry it solo. Say it out loud to one person instead of white-knuckling it alone.
