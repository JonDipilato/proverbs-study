Agur isn't asking to be poor for humility points, and he isn't asking to be rich for security. He asks for enough — because he's honest that too much makes him cocky ("Who is the LORD?") and too little makes him a thief. He's not smarter or holier than money can bend, and neither are you. The ask isn't "make me strong enough to handle either extreme." It's "keep me out of the extremes in the first place."
