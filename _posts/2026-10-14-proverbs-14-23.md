This verse is blunt about something most people would rather not hear: work leaves you richer, and talk doesn't. Not "talk is a waste of time" — talk actually tends toward *less*, toward running short. You can spend an hour explaining your plan, hyping up an idea, telling everybody what you're about to do, and when you're done talking you've got exactly what you started with, minus the hour. This verse says it's worse than that — all that talk actually drains you. Every minute spent describing the work instead of doing it is a minute you could've put into something that pays off. The guy who just does the job, even the boring parts, walks away with more than he had. The guy who just talks about the job walks away with less. If you've got more mouth than hands on something today, flip it before the day's over.
