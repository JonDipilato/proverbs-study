This isn't a verse about staying away from obviously evil people wearing a sign. It's about the road that leads to profiting off other people's pain — cutting a corner at work, taking advantage of someone who doesn't know better, letting a bad habit or a bad crowd keep you up at night chasing an edge. Solomon doesn't say "resist it when it shows up." He says get off that road entirely, before you're anywhere near it. Waiting until you're standing right next to the temptation to decide what to do is already too late — the decision has to happen now, at a distance.
