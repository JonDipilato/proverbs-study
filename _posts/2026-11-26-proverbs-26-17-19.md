---
Two kinds of reckless, same bad ending. The first guy in this passage sees a fight that has nothing to do with him and jumps in anyway — Solomon says that's as smart as grabbing a strange dog by the ears. You're not helping. You're about to get bit, and it was never your fight to begin with.

The second picture is worse because it's quieter. A madman waving fire around is obviously dangerous — everybody steps back. But the man who hurts you with his words or his actions and then says "relax, I was just joking" is doing the exact same damage without the warning. He gets to call it harmless because he smiled when he did it. You still got burned.

Both verses are about people who won't own what they're actually doing. One hides behind "I was just trying to help." The other hides behind "I was just kidding." Solomon strips the cover off both. If it caused real damage, the label you put on it afterward doesn't matter.

Watch where you insert yourself today, and watch what you let yourself say under the cover of a laugh. Neither one is as harmless as it feels in the moment.
