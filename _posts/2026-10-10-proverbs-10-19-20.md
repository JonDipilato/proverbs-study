word_note: "*Chasak* isn't \"can't think of anything to say.\" It's a man actively grabbing hold of words that are loaded in the chamber and choosing not to fire them. The verse doesn't say talking is sin — it says the more words you put out, the more surface area sin has to land on. The guy who stays wise is the one holding the leash on his own mouth. The Hebrew is literally \"silver, chosen\" — silver that's been picked out, tested, and set apart from the scrap. That's what the just man's tongue is: words that have been through a selection process before they leave his mouth, not whatever came to mind first. The wicked man's heart, by contrast, is \"little worth\" — cheap, unrefined, not worth the weight."

He's not asking you to go silent. He's asking you to run your words through a filter before they go out — the way a refiner runs silver through fire to burn off the junk. Less talking, more weighing. A mouth that never stops running is a mouth that eventually runs into sin; a mouth that holds back and picks its words is a mouth you can trust.
