---
Verse 1 isn't abstract — it's a handshake deal. You backed a friend's debt, put your own name behind a stranger's money. Verse 2 names the cost before you even feel it: you are snared, caught, and the trap wasn't set by the other guy. It was built out of your own mouth, your own words, the promise you made without thinking it through.

Verse 3 is the way out, and Solomon doesn't soften it. Tear yourself loose. Go be the one asking instead of the one owed. Humble yourself — stamp your pride down and go deal with the person face to face, today, even if it's awkward or embarrassing.

Verse 4 shuts the door on waiting. No sleep until it's handled. Not "deal with it when you're less stressed." Not "bring it up next time you see them." Tonight, before your head hits the pillow, if something you said with your mouth has you on the hook, go say something else with your mouth to get loose.
