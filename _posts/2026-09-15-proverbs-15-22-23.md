These two verses go together. Verse 22 says: don't decide big stuff solo — run it past someone first, because your own head is a blind spot you can't see out of. Verse 23 says: even when you've got the right words, hold them until the moment is actually right. A good answer dropped at the wrong second can do more damage than saying nothing. Wisdom here isn't just having the right take — it's checking your take with someone else, and then waiting for the right second to say it.
