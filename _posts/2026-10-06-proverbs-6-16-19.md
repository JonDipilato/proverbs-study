Look at the shape of the list: a proud look, a lying tongue, violent hands, a plotting heart, feet running toward trouble, a false witness, somebody sowing discord. Four of the seven are about speech or relationships. God isn't just watching what you do to people with your hands — He's watching what you do to them with your mouth and your version of events. He's asking you to be a person whose word can be trusted and who doesn't go looking for a fight.
