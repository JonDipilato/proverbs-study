Three moves, in order. First, *zamam*: think it through before you commit — run the numbers, don't just feel it out. Second, gird your arms: once you've decided, work it for real, not halfway. Third, *ta'am*: don't call it a win until you've actually checked the result against reality. A lot of guys skip straight from "feels right" to "I'm in," and skip checking altogether once they're in. This woman does neither. She thinks first, works hard, and then tests — and keeps testing, even late at night when it'd be easier to just assume it's fine.
