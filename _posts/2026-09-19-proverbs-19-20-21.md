You've got a plan for today, this week, this year. Fine — you should. But you're not required to run it solo, and you're not guaranteed it survives contact with reality. The guy who won't take advice and won't hold his plans loose is setting himself up to get blindsided. The guy who lets other people talk into his plan, and leaves room for God to override it, is the one who's actually wise by the time it's all over.
