These two verses go together. Verse 9 says: when someone wrongs you and it's been dealt with, bury it — don't keep digging it up to win points or vent to other people. That's how you keep a friend. Verse 10 says the flip side: when someone corrects YOU, don't get defensive and make them say it a hundred different ways. Hear it once, take it, change. Covering other people's stuff and receiving correction on your own stuff are the same muscle — humility instead of ego.
