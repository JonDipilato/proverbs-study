Solomon isn't anti-work. He's anti-worship — anti making money the reason you get up, the thing you gasp for, the thing you trust to keep you safe. The moment you're leaning on "my own wisdom" (your hustle, your grind, your plan) instead of God, you're already exhausted chasing something that can be gone by lunch. Work hard. Just don't run yourself into the ground for something that flies.
