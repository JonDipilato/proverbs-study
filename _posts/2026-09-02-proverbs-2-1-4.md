Nobody stumbles into wisdom by accident. You don't drift into knowing how to run your money, raise your kids, or handle your temper — you go dig it out on purpose, the same way you'd go after a raise or a good deal on a truck. Verse 1 says receive it and hide it in you — don't let it bounce off. Verse 2 says point your ear and your heart at it on purpose. Verse 3 says cry out for it — ask for it like you actually want it. Verse 4 is the payoff line: treat wisdom like silver you're hunting and treasure you're digging for, not like a podcast playing in the background while you scroll.
