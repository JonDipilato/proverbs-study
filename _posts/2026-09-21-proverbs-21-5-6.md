These two verses are one thought. The guy who won't slow down to plan is the same guy who'll lie to get paid faster — impatience and dishonesty come from the same place: not wanting to wait for the real thing. Diligence means you sit down and actually think through how the money, the job, or the deal is supposed to work before you move. Hasty means you skip that step because waiting feels like losing. And when you skip it, the shortcut that gets you paid today is usually a lie you told somebody — a stretched invoice, a promise you can't keep, a number that isn't quite true. It feels like cash in your hand. It's actually a breath. It slips through your fingers because it was never really yours.
