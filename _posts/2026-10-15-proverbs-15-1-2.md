---
These two verses are about the same half-second: the gap between hearing something and answering it. Verse 1 says the words you pick in that gap decide whether the thing cools down or blows up. A soft answer isn't backing down — it's choosing a controlled response over a hard one, and it actually turns wrath away. Grievous words do the opposite; they don't just fail to help, they stir the anger up higher. Verse 2 goes one layer deeper and asks why some people answer soft and others answer sharp. It's not luck and it's not personality — it's whether you're actually using what you know, or just letting whatever's loudest in your head come out your mouth. The wise man's tongue aims his knowledge like a tool. The fool's mouth has no aim at all — it just gushes, whatever's in there, all over whoever's standing closest. Most blow-ups today won't come from someone being evil. They'll come from someone just letting it pour out instead of aiming it.
