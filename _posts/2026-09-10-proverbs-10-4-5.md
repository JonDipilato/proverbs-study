A slack hand isn't just "lazy," it's a hidden way of letting people down — your family, your boss, your customers, even yourself. And diligence isn't grinding all the time on everything; it's being sharp enough to see the harvest while it's happening and move on it, instead of sleeping through the one window you had. Poverty and shame in this passage aren't bad luck. They're what's left over after you let the moment pass.
