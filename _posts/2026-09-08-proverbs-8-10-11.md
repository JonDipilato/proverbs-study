This isn't a guilt trip about loving money. Wisdom isn't anti-cash — a few verses later she brags her own fruit beats gold too. This is about what you reach for first when you can only grab one thing. Cash and rubies get spent, get stolen, or just sit in a box. Getting corrected right — actually hearing it and changing — is the one thing you keep for good. Most guys will take the raise and dodge the correction. Wisdom says do the opposite: take the correction like it's worth more, because it is.
