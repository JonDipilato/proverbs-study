Verses 15-16 aren't about being stingy with your marriage — they're about not leaking your attention, your time, and your body somewhere it doesn't belong. Verse 17 says plainly: keep it yours, keep it home. Verse 18 flips from warning to blessing — rejoice with the wife of your youth, on purpose, today, not as a leftover after everything else gets your energy. A well that nobody draws from goes stale. A marriage that gets no attention starts to feel like the thing you outgrew, and that feeling is exactly what temptation uses to sell you something worse.
