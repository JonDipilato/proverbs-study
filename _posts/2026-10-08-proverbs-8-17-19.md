Wisdom isn't shy here — she's making you an offer. Get up and go after her on purpose, the way you'd get up early for a job you actually wanted, and she pays out: not just feelings, but "durable riches" — the kind that doesn't rot, get stolen, or crash with the market. Gold and silver are the comparison because gold and silver are what everybody already trusts. Wisdom's claim is that she beats them. That's a real claim you can test with your own life, not a nice verse to frame on a wall.
