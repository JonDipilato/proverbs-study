Verse 7 sounds like setup, but it's not optional: hear this, and don't drift from it. Then verse 8 gets specific fast. It doesn't tell you to resist her when you're standing at the door. It tells you to never get near the door in the first place — *harchek*, put real distance between your path and hers. That's the whole strategy. Not willpower at the moment of temptation, but refusing to be anywhere close to the moment at all.

Verses 9 and 10 are why it's worth being that extreme. This isn't framed as "you might feel bad." It's framed as a transaction you don't get to undo: your honor goes to someone else, your years go to a person with zero mercy, and your strength — the actual output of your working life — ends up spent in a house that isn't yours. The cost isn't a moment of regret. It's years, collected by someone who won't cut you a break.

So the move isn't "try harder next time you're tempted." It's "don't be there." Name the actual door in your life — the app, the person, the time of night, the conversation — and put a real barrier between you and it today, before you need willpower you might not have in the moment.
