You've got a "strong tower" whether you like it or not — some place you run to first when you're scared. For a lot of guys it's the bank balance, the resume, the emergency fund, the side hustle. Proverbs 18 isn't saying money is evil or that savings are dumb. It's saying: don't confuse the wall you built with the tower that actually holds. One is real stone under God's name. The other is only as strong as you imagine it is — and imagination cracks the day the number in the account doesn't match the size of the problem.
