Some people talk before they think, and it cuts people like a blade — fast, sharp, and it leaves a mark. Other people think before they talk, and their words actually heal something. And here's the kicker on truth (v.19): a lie feels powerful for about five minutes, then it's gone — but a true word you said stands forever, because nobody ever has to walk it back.
