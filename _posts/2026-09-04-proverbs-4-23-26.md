Stand guard on your own heart like it's a wall somebody could climb over — because it is. What you let sit in there long enough eventually comes out your mouth, shows up in where your eyes wander, and shapes the path your feet actually walk. This isn't "think positive." It's: whatever you're feeding your heart today is the water that's going to come out of the tap tomorrow, so watch the well, not just the faucet.
