Stop waiting for someone to make you move. The ant proves that the work still needs doing whether or not anyone's checking on you. If you only produce when the boss is looking, the client is waiting, or someone's about to notice, you're not actually diligent — you're just afraid of getting caught being lazy. God's asking for the other kind: the kind that works because the work is real, not because someone's watching.
