Cosigning a loan, backing a friend's business debt, putting your name on someone else's obligation — that's *arab*. It feels generous in the moment. Solomon says look two steps ahead: if that person can't pay, the debt doesn't disappear, it walks straight to your door. He's not against generosity. Give the man cash if you've got it to lose. Don't give him your signature, because your signature can take your bed.
