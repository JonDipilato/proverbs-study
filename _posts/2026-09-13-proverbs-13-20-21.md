This isn't about being cold to people who mess up. It's about who sets your pace every single day — who you eat lunch with, who's blowing up your phone, whose opinion actually moves you. Verse 21 backs it up: trouble tracks down people who keep bad company on its own, without you chasing it. You don't have to do anything wrong yourself — just keep grazing in the wrong field long enough, and the field changes you.
