You don't run tomorrow. You don't run how good you look, either. Both of those get proven by what actually happens and what other people actually say — not by you talking first. Every time you talk like a deal is done before it's done, or talk yourself up before anyone else has, you're grabbing something that isn't yours to grab yet.
