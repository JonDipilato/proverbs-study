This verse isn't really about merchants and scales — it's about every number you hand someone today that they can't easily double-check. Your invoice. Your time card. The hours you say you worked. What you tell a buyer about the thing you're selling. God puts "cheated by half an ounce" in the same bucket as the stuff you'd call a real sin, and He puts "told the full, accurate truth about a number" in the same bucket as the stuff that makes Him happy. He's not grading your church attendance here — He's grading your math.
