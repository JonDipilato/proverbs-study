A bird won't walk into a trap it can see. You will — every time you take the shady shortcut because the payout looks good right now. The guy who skims off a client, fudges an invoice, or takes the "too easy" cash job isn't getting over on anyone. He's the bird flying straight at the net, except this time nobody even had to hide it from him. He saw it coming and went anyway, because the money blinded him to the trap.
