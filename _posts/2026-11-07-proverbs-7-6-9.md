This guy isn't unlucky. He's not minding his business and temptation just showed up. He picked the street. He picked the hour. He walked there on purpose, one deliberate step at a time, and then waited for the dark to cover him so nobody he knew would see.

That's the lie about temptation most guys believe: it just happens to you. It doesn't. You drive past her house a different way for no reason. You "happen" to be on your phone at 11pm when you know who's going to text. You stay late at work for a meeting that isn't actually required. Nobody trips and falls into sin. People walk there, one choice at a time, usually timed for when nobody's watching.

The fix isn't willpower in the moment. By the time you're standing at her corner at midnight, the fight is basically already lost. The fix is earlier — don't take the first step down that street at all. Don't open that chat. Don't drive that road. Don't give yourself an excuse to be alone at that hour with that person. You're not weak for avoiding it, you're smart for seeing where your own feet are headed before they get there.
