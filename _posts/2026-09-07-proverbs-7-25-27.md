The fight isn't won or lost at the bedroom door. It's won or lost in the two seconds when your heart first leans toward something you know you shouldn't want. By the time you're standing in the doorway arguing with yourself, you've already lost — the *lev* turned aside back when it was easy to stop. And the payoff at the end of that road isn't a fun secret, it's a real grave: a marriage, a family, a reputation, a conscience, all buried. Solomon isn't exaggerating for effect. He's naming the actual address.
