God isn't asking you to be poor. He's asking you to prove, with your money, that He's actually first — not first in your head, first in your bank app. *Reshith* means the giving happens before the spending, not after. Most guys plan to give "whatever's left" at the end of the month. There's never anything left. This verse flips the order: pay God first, off the top, then live on what's left — and verse 10 says that's the man whose barns end up full, not empty.
