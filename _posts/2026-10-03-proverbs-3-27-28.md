This isn't about being generous with money you don't have. It's about the money, the favor, the answer, or the apology you DO have sitting in your hand right now — and you're stalling anyway. Maybe it's awkward. Maybe you just don't feel like dealing with it today. Solomon's not buying it. If it's in your power right now, "tomorrow" is a lie you're telling yourself to buy comfort.
