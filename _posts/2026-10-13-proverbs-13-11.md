Everybody's seen both kinds of money. The guy who wins big or finds an angle and has nothing to show for it a year later. And the guy doing the same unglamorous work week after week who quietly ends up solid. This verse says that's not luck — it's built into how the two kinds of money behave. Money that shows up without real work behind it doesn't just risk running out, it actively dwindles, like it's leaking. Money gathered by labor — one handful, one shift, one invoice at a time — doesn't just hold steady, it multiplies. If you're waiting on a break today, stop. Go pick up the next handful instead.
