Pride doesn't wait for the fall to show up — it does its damage the moment it swells (*ga'own*). By the time you're actually flat on your face, the real break already happened earlier, in your head, when you started thinking you were above the room. Verse 19 is the trade God is offering: you can stay low and plain (*shaphal*) with regular people, or you can stand tall over a bigger pile with people who think they're better than everyone else. God says the small, humble pile wins. Not because money or wins are bad — because the swelling that comes with them is what actually kills you.
