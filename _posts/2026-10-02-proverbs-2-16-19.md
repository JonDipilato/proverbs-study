This isn't just a warning about one kind of sin. It's a warning about smooth words in general — the pitch that sounds too easy, the person who tells you exactly what you want to hear, the deal that's been sanded down so you don't feel any of the rough edges you should be feeling. Verse 18 doesn't say her path *might* lead somewhere bad. It says her house already leans toward death, right now, before you ever walk in. And verse 19 is the hard part: this isn't a detour you can just walk back from whenever you feel like it. Some roads don't loop back to the main road. Wisdom's whole move here isn't "be strong enough to resist in the moment" — it's "see the smoothness for what it is before you're standing in the house."
