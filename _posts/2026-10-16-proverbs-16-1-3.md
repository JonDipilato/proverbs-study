These three verses go together like a diagnosis and a cure. Verse 1 says even the words on your tongue aren't fully your own call — the answer comes from the LORD. Verse 2 is the diagnosis: you think your ways are clean, and "clean in his own eyes" is the exact problem. That's not an insult, it's just how self-judgment works — you grade your own motives on a curve nobody else can see. So God doesn't weigh the outcome. He weighs the spirit, the actual intent underneath the action. Verse 3 is the cure: don't try to out-argue your own blind spot. Roll the work onto Him instead — not "pray about it and then grip it just as tight," but actually hand it over, the way you'd roll a heavy stone off your own hands onto someone stronger. And the payoff isn't a feeling, it's a result: your thoughts get established, they hold up under pressure instead of falling apart the first time something goes sideways. Most plans don't fail because the strategy was bad. They fail because the guy running them was gripping the wheel so tight he couldn't see what was actually in front of him. Loosen the grip today. Roll it onto Him. Then go to work.
